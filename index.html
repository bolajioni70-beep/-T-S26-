Our Story

I still remember it — freshman year, phone battery dead, just needed somewhere to charge it. There was this guy running the charging spot, already a big man on campus (400 level, while I was just a fresher finding my way around)...

He plugged in my phone, asked my name, introduced himself, and told me if I ever needed anything, I should come find him.

I did. He helped me clean up my hostel room, showed me how to survive school life, and whenever I missed home, he'd invite me over for proper home-cooked meals...

Life happened after school — we lost touch for a while. Then one day, we ran into each other again... at the gym, of all places. Turns out I was exactly what he needed.

"And now? That same guy who once called me his little sister... is about to call me his wife."
